04 / A FEW HELPFUL DETAILS

Before we
begin.

Can I order smaller matching albums?+

Yes. Pair a main album with smaller copies in the same cover and finish. A 30 × 30 cm album with two 20 × 20 cm albums is one example.

Which sizes and spread counts are available?+

Choose square or landscape formats, including 30 × 30, 40 × 30, 35 × 25 and 30 × 20 cm. The quote form includes 10–35 spreads, plus an option to discuss other requirements. One spread is two facing pages.

How can I personalise the cover?+

Choose a material and colour, then add foil lettering, a picture window or UV-printed artwork. Include names, a date or spine wording in your enquiry.

Do you work with photographers?+

Yes. We make client albums and studio samples, with personal help choosing formats, materials and finishes to complement your photography.

Do you make wedding magazines?+

Yes. We also offer a lighter, editorial format for your wedding photographs. Select Wedding magazine in our quote form to tell us what you have in mind.

How do I request a quote?+

Choose your album size, spreads, covers, matching copies and presentation boxes. Our form brings everything together, ready to send to us by WhatsApp or email.
