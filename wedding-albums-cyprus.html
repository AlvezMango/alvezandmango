Wedding albums, handmade in Cyprus.

Your wedding photographs deserve a place you can return to. We create bespoke wedding albums in Nicosia, combining rigid lay-flat pages with carefully chosen covers and a design that puts your photographs first.

A spread that opens flat. A story that stays together.

Our albums use hard, mounted pages approximately 1–1.2 mm thick. The lay-flat binding lets an open spread rest flat, giving your photographs room across both pages. These are substantial album pages with a firm feel in the hand.

From a quiet portrait to a full wedding scene, the album format gives each part of the day its own space. Choose the size and number of spreads around the photographs you want to keep.

Your album. A smaller one to share.

A 30 × 30 cm wedding album can be paired with one or two 20 × 20 cm parent albums. A popular starting point is 25 spreads in each album — 50 facing pages — with coordinated covers for a complete set.

Tell us whether you need a single album or a set, your preferred size and spread count. We will quote for your chosen specification.

Choose a cover that feels like you.

Explore linen, cotton, faux leather and suede in our cover material collection. Colour, texture and finish give the album its character before the first page is opened.

For cover personalisation or a particular combination of materials, include your idea when you enquire so we can discuss the available options.

Planning your wedding album

What is a spread?

A spread is the two facing pages you see when the album is open. An album with 25 spreads has 50 facing pages.

Can I order matching parent albums?

Yes. Smaller 20 × 20 cm albums can accompany a 30 × 30 cm main album. Tell us how many copies you need and which covers you prefer.

Can photographers enquire?

Yes. We work with photographers on albums and coordinated sets. Contact us with your requirements, or use the photographer login if you already have an account.

What should I include in a quote request?

Send your album size, number of spreads, number of copies and preferred cover material. If you have a date in mind, include it so we can confirm timing for your order.

Where are you based?

We are in Nicosia, Cyprus. Find our contact and studio details on the contact page.
